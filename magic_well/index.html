🔎 Bonus: Whose…? / his · hers · theirs

Light extension from the language-focus page (not the main drill).

Whose backpack is this?

It's mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs.

Whose watches are these?

They're mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs.

Adjective → Pronoun

  • my thing(s) → mine
  • your thing(s) → yours
  • his thing(s) → his
  • her thing(s) → hers
  • our thing(s) → ours
  • their thing(s) → theirs

Tony's bag → “It's his.” / My bag → “It's mine.”
