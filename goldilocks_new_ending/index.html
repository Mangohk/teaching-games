Sentence Formula Feelings + Reasons

Pattern: Feelings + "because..."

Example 1 (The Bears):

"Baby Bear was furious because his chair was broken."

Example 2 (Goldilocks):

"Goldilocks was terrified because three huge bears stood over her."

💡 Adding "because..." makes your sentence longer and gives reasons!

Ending Choice A Idea 1

Path A: The Helpful Fixer  🛠️

🛠️🍲

Apologize and Repair

She stays and makes things right.

1. Goldilocks stays and apologizes politely.

2. She uses glue to fix Baby Bear's broken chair.

3. She cooks fresh warm porridge with sweet berries.

✍️ Write these ideas in the past tense.

💡 Moral: Always make things right when you make a mistake.

Writing Scaffold

Supported Story Frame (94 Words) 📝

🐻🚪When the bears came home, they found the empty bowls and the broken chair. They went upstairs and saw Goldilocks in Baby Bear's bed.

😢Suddenly, Baby Bear shouted loudly. Goldilocks was as timid as a mouse because three angry bears were staring at her.

🛠️Instead of running away, she stood up and said, "I am very sorry!" She helped fix the chair and cooked warm food.

💡Finally, the bears forgave her. She learned that she must always knock and ask before touching others' things.

Total: 94 Words • Easily passes the 80-word minimum!
Advanced Extension

5-Point Challenge 🚀

1️⃣ Sensory Details: Describe Papa Bear's rumbling growl.

2️⃣ Vivid Verbs: Use stammered, confessed, gasped.

3️⃣ Rich Dialogue: Include 2 lines of well-punctuated speech.

4️⃣ 2 Similes: e.g., as timid as a mouse AND as busy as a bee.

5️⃣ Deep Moral: Explain how she changed her behavior forever.

Aim for 100–120 words! See the model on the next slide ➔
High-Level Model 105 Words

Read Our Model Ending Together 📖

🐻🚪When the bears came home, they discovered their ruined porridge and cracked furniture. Suddenly, Baby Bear pointed and gasped, "Look! A girl is sleeping in my bed!"

😢Goldilocks awoke instantly. She was as timid as a mouse under Papa Bear's glare. However, she confessed politely, "I was hungry and lost. Please forgive me."

🛠️💡She was as busy as a bee, sweeping the floor and fixing the chair. Finally, the bears smiled warmly. From this incident, she learned that we must always respect other people's homes and ask before using their things.

✔ Prompt Starter ✔ 2 Animal Similes ✔ Clear Moral Total: 105 Words
