Station 1: Manner Charades Cut-Out Prompt Cards (12 Cards)

Cut along dashed lines. Fold or place into an envelope. Students draw one card and act silently!

🚌 🧘 wait patiently

Act: Stand calmly in line with a relaxed smile and hands neatly folded.

🚌 😤 waitfile impatiently

Act: Tap foot rapidly, puff cheeks, check watch angrily, try to jump the queue.

🍽️ 😊 speak politely

Act: Smile warmly at waiter, bow slightly, mouth "Please could I have water".

🍽️ 😠 speak rudely

Act: Slam the table with fist, point an angry finger, demand food with a frown.

📱 📢 speak / talk loudly

Act: Hold phone to ear, pace around shouting with wide eyes and huge gestures.

🤫 📚 speak / whisper softly

Act: Cup hand around ear/mouth, tiptoe between library bookshelves, signal "shh!".

⚽ 🎈 play happily

Act: Laugh cheerfully, throw an imaginary ball, jump with joyous energy!

🧑‍🏫 🧘 teach patiently

Act: Point gently at chalkboard, nod encouragingly, guide a struggling learner.

🎒 ⏰ go to school punctually

Act: Walk calmly through school gate, check watch: exactly 7:55 AM (on time!).

🍳 ⭐ cook well

Act: Flip pancake with a pan, taste with a spoon, kiss fingers: "Delicious!"

📖 🤫 read quietly

Act: Hold an open book gently, turn pages silently, completely absorbed in reading.

✍️ ⚡ do homework quickly / fast

Act: Write furiously across paper with turbo speed, turn pages at lightning pace!
