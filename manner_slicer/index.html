Vocabulary & Grammar Focus

Golden Grammar Rule:

Adjectives describe nouns (people, things): "She is a careful driver."

Adverbs of Manner describe verbs (how actions happen): "She drives carefully."

⭐ Chameleon Starfruits (Both): fast, high, far, late, early can be BOTH adjectives and adverbs!

1. Regular: Add "-ly" to Adjective

polite ➔ politely | patient ➔ patiently | quiet ➔ quietly | rude ➔ rudely | soft ➔ softly | loud ➔ loudly | quick ➔ quickly | slow ➔ slowly | kind ➔ kindly | neat ➔ neatly | careful ➔ carefully

2. Spelling Change: "-y" becomes "-ily"

noisy ➔ noisily | messy ➔ messily | easy ➔ easily | tidy ➔ tidily

3. Irregular Transformation

good (adjective) ➔ well (adverb of manner)

4. Both Adjective and Adverb (No change!)

fast | high | far | late | early
