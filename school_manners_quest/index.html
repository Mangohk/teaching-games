📜 How to Play School Manners Quest

1️⃣

Roll the 6-Sided Dice: Rolls 1, 2, 3, 4, 😊 Happy Face, or ⭐ Super Star.

2️⃣

Numbers (1-4): Answer 1 question from 5 types (Adverb, Should/Shouldn't, In order to/not to, Verb collocations, or Find the Mistake). Correct = move that number!

3️⃣

😊 Happy Face: Appoint any other group to act out a manner with an adverb. Move +1 step free (no questions needed)!

4️⃣

⭐ Super Star: Whole class stands up to do the manner action! The teacher selects teams that performed correctly to advance +2 steps!

5️⃣

Wrong Answer = Mime Forfeit: No movement! The representative must perform the funny manner mime!
