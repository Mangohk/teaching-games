The Golden Grammar Rules

🏃 What is a VERB?

A verb is an action word! It tells what someone or something does.

Test: Can you "do" it right now? Yes! (jump, dance, investigate)

🎨 What is an ADJECTIVE?

An adjective is a describing word! It tells what kind, color, size, or shape something is.

Test: Can you describe a puppy with it? Yes! (happy, sparkly, mysterious)
